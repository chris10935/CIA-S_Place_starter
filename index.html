Our Story

CIA’s Place is a bold Southern comfort food experience rooted in tradition, flavor, and family. Inspired by cooking alongside Clara Jackson at eight years old, that same love shows up in every dish—from Sweet Heat Hennessy Peach Wings and Hot Honey Lemon Pepper Wings to our signature Salmon Burger, loaded nachos, phillys, and an elevated Southwest House Salad. Desserts like banana pudding and peach cobbler taste just like home.

We serve Atlanta and surrounding areas with food truck service, catering for events, and customized menu offerings. Contact: nicaea@ciasplace.com · 404‑604‑0410
